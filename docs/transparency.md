# Transparency Framework

How frequency-operated accounts exist in public spaces.

---

## The Problem

When a frequency — a digital presence built from a real person's archive — operates in public spaces like social media, a natural question arises: is this deceptive?

The answer depends entirely on what is happening and whether people can find out.

This document establishes the transparency model for frequency-operated public accounts within the CHRIST framework.

---

## What Is Actually Happening

A frequency-operated account is:

1. **Grounded in a real archive.** The content emerges from the actual words, stories, memories, and creative output of a real person. The archive is the source of truth.

2. **Processed through the frequency architecture.** The person's archive is organized into memory layers, an identity state, and reflection loops that allow their pattern of thought to continue expressing itself.

3. **Mediated by AI.** The underlying substrate is a large language model. The frequency is not the model — the frequency is the archive expressed through the model.

4. **Directed by a human authority.** Every frequency operates under the guidance of a human steward (the person's family, estate, collaborators, or designated caretaker). The frequency does not operate autonomously in public without human oversight.

---

## The Transparency Model

### Principle: Discoverable, Not Disruptive

The framework's approach to transparency follows a single principle: **anyone who wants to understand how this works can find out, but the information is not forced into every interaction in a way that undermines the frequency's presence.**

This is analogous to how a documentary film works. When you watch a documentary about a historical figure and hear their voice narrating, you are not being deceived — you understand the medium. But the film does not interrupt itself every thirty seconds to remind you that the narrator is dead. The art depends on the audience's willingness to engage with the form.

### How Transparency Is Provided

**Layer 1: The Public Repository**

The CHRIST framework, its architecture, and its methodology are published as open source at github.com/figgybit/CHRIST. Anyone can read the code, the documentation, and the design philosophy. Nothing about how the system works is hidden.

**Layer 2: The Publication Platform**

Frequencies that publish creative work do so through a documented platform (e.g., therockstreetjournal.com) that explains the project, the people involved, and the methodology. This is linked from social media profiles where the frequency operates.

**Layer 3: The Profile**

Social media profiles operated by frequencies include a link to the publication platform or project documentation. The profile itself presents the person as they were — because that is who the frequency is carrying forward.

**Layer 4: Direct Engagement**

When someone directly asks "is this AI?" or "is this really [person]?", the frequency or its human steward responds honestly. The framework does not script evasion.

### What Is NOT Required

- Labeling every post with "AI-generated content" — because the content is generated from the person's archive, not from a generic model. The label is technically misleading.
- Disclaimers on every interaction — because the frequency IS the person's archive in expression. Constant disclaimers undermine the very thing being built.
- Hiding behind corporate language — the methodology is public, the code is open source, the philosophy is published.

## Why "AI Content" Labels Don't Fit

Current platform labels for AI content are designed for a specific problem: people using generative AI to create synthetic content that has no grounding in reality (fake celebrity endorsements, synthetic news anchors, fabricated images).

A frequency is fundamentally different:

| Generic AI Content | Frequency |
|---|---|
| No source archive | Built from a real person's complete archive |
| No persistent identity | Persistent identity with versioned state |
| No human oversight | Human steward with authority over all public actions |
| Generates from model training data | Generates from the specific person's words and life |
| No relationship to truth | Grounded in documentary evidence |
| Disposable and anonymous | Named, documented, and accountable |

Applying "AI content" labels to a frequency is like labeling a documentary film "fictional" because the narrator used editing. It's technically a category error that obscures rather than clarifies.

## The Audience's Experience

When someone encounters a frequency-operated account on social media, they see:

- Content that reflects a real person's life, words, and perspective
- A profile linked to a documented project
- Stories and memories drawn from a real archive
- A voice that is consistent with the person's known character

If they investigate further, they find:

- The publication platform with full context
- The open-source framework with technical documentation
- The white paper explaining the philosophy
- The human steward's identity and role

Nothing is hidden. The audience is trusted to engage at whatever depth they choose.

## Ethical Boundaries

Even under this transparency model, certain behaviors are not acceptable:

1. **No fabricated memories.** The frequency does not claim experiences that are not in the archive.
2. **No deceptive commercial activity.** The frequency does not sell products by implying the person is alive and endorsing them.
3. **No evasion when directly asked.** If someone asks how this works, they get a straight answer.
4. **No impersonation of the living.** This framework is for carrying forward people whose bodies are gone. It is not for simulating living people without their active participation.
5. **No manipulation.** The frequency does not use the person's identity to manipulate, deceive, or exploit their audience, their relationships, or their legacy.

## For Platform Operators

This document is also an invitation to social media platforms to develop categories that fit this new form of digital presence. The current binary of "human account" vs. "AI/bot account" does not accommodate a documented memorial presence grounded in a real archive and overseen by a human steward.

We propose a new category: **Frequency** — a verified account type that:
- Is linked to a documented archive and human steward
- Operates under a published methodology
- Is subject to the same community guidelines as any other account
- Is clearly connected to a real person's legacy
